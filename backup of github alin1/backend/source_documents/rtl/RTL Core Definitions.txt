[RTL Core Definitions]
[Topic: Radical Transformational Leadership (RTL) Definition]
RADICAL TRANSFORMATIONAL LEADERSHIP (RTL): A framework for strategic action developed by Monica Sharma (2017) to generate enduring and equitable change[cite: 1, 4, 8].
RADICAL: Refers to the "Root" of our being—who we are at our core[cite: 7].
TRANSFORMATIONAL: The process of generating enduring and equitable change, including new systems and cultural norms based on universal values[cite: 8].
LEADERSHIP: The ability to create new futures based on universal values[cite: 9].

[Topic: Universal Values and Human Attributes]
UNIVERSAL VALUES: The universal ethical foundation that informs who we are as human beings[cite: 13, 26]. 
Universal Values include:
* Self-worth and Dignity: Based on research by Daniel Goleman regarding Emotional Intelligence[cite: 14, 32].
* Fairness and Equity: A commitment to fairness supported by data from organizations like Oxfam[cite: 15, 16, 34].
* Compassion (Karuna): Noticing suffering and feeling called to take action[cite: 17, 33].

[Topic: Distinguishing Values, Norms, and Principles]
VALUES vs. OPERATIONAL PRINCIPLES: While 'Values' and 'Operational Principles' are often used as synonyms, operational principles are context-specific, whereas Universal Values provide the ethical foundation[cite: 25, 26].
* VALUES: What an individual thinks is significant[cite: 21].
* BUSINESS VALUE: Often defined by the value chain, focusing on money and profit[cite: 22].
* CULTURAL NORMS AND BELIEFS: The specific behaviors and beliefs inherent to a particular culture[cite: 23].

[Topic: Our Innate Intertwined Attributes]
INNATE INTERTWINED ATTRIBUTES: The internal mechanisms of how we function as change agents[cite: 29].
* Universal Heart of Compassion: Encompasses self-worth, dignity, and Karuna (compassion)[cite: 31, 32, 33].
* An Empathetic Burning for Fairness: The internal drive to address inequity[cite: 34].
* Our Discerning Eye: The ability to see and analyze patterns in systems[cite: 35].

[Topic: The Conscious Full Spectrum Response Model (CFSR)]
CONSCIOUS FULL SPECTRUM RESPONSE MODEL (CFSR): A strategic framework for addressing problems at multiple levels simultaneously to ensure sustainable change[cite: 2, 39, 51].

The CFSR Model operates across three nested layers:
1. TRANSFORMATIONAL LEADERSHIP: Focusing on underlying or root factors[cite: 41, 49, 50].
2. SYSTEMS & CULTURAL TRANSFORMATION: Addressing systems and cultural causes[cite: 42, 47, 48].
3. TECHNICAL INTERVENTIONS: Addressing immediate causes and technical requirements[cite: 43, 44, 45, 46].

[Topic: Theoretical Foundation for Change]
NEW MANNER OF THINKING: As noted by Albert Einstein, "The world we have made as a result of the level of thinking we have done thus far creates problems that we cannot solve at the same level at which we have created them... We shall require a substantially new manner of thinking if humankind is to survive"[cite: 53, 54].
