[Sourcing Inner Capacity]
[Topic: Sourcing Inner Capacity - Definition]
INNER CAPACITY: That which an individual cares deeply about, specifically the Universal Values they stand for and which guide them[cite: 83]. Sourcing inner capacity is the act of utilizing these Universal Values for strategic action[cite: 88]. It is achieved through self-awareness by knowing who I am, what I think, and what I do[cite: 84, 85, 86, 87].

[Topic: Universal Values vs. Other Value Types]
UNIVERSAL VALUES: The universal ethical foundation that serves as our real strength[cite: 99, 104].
* Values vs. Operational Principles: While often used as synonyms, operational principles are context-specific, whereas Universal Values are foundational[cite: 97, 98].
* Business Values: Often defined by the "Value Chain," focusing on money and profit[cite: 95].
* Cultural Norms and Beliefs: Contextual assumptions based on the specific culture one belongs to[cite: 96].

[Topic: Being 'Unmessablewith']
UNMESSABLEWITH: A state of being achieved by sourcing inner capacities and universal values[cite: 102, 103]. It represents real strength, as opposed to domination, bullying, or ridicule, which are signs of inner weakness[cite: 104, 105]. Because no one can take inner capacity away, being 'unmessablewith' allows an individual to bounce back from obstacles[cite: 106, 107].

[Topic: Understanding and Transcending Fear]
SOCIALIZED FEARS: Normal fears that form as individuals grow up; they are not the same as real-life threats (e.g., assault) or clinical phobias[cite: 113, 114, 116, 117].
COURAGE: Not the absence of fear, but the ability to act in spite of it[cite: 120].
The process for transcending fear to source Universal Values involves four steps:
1. Notice the fear[cite: 121].
2. Name the fear[cite: 122].
3. Let go/Release the fear[cite: 123].
4. Be in action sourcing Universal Values[cite: 124].

[Topic: Focus and Growth of Inner Capacity]
GROWTH OF ATTENTION: As a conscious human being, whatever an individual gives attention to grows[cite: 134]. Focusing on fears causes them to grow, while focusing on inner capacities and what one stands for causes those capacities to grow[cite: 135, 136].

[Topic: Six Steps for Sourcing Inner Capacity for Action]
SIX STEPS FOR STRATEGIC ACTION:
1. Know who I am as a possibility—what I stand for[cite: 141, 142].
2. Declare my stand—my Universal Values[cite: 142].
3. Know my fears[cite: 143].
4. Transcend my fear—Be 'unmessablewith'[cite: 144].
5. Commit to action[cite: 145].
6. Source my and others' Universal Values for strategic action[cite: 146].

[Topic: Inspiration for Purpose]
PURPOSE AND WILL: As stated by Chief Seattle, when one knows who they are and their mission is clear, burning with the inner fire of an unbreakable will, no cold can touch the heart and no deluge can dampen their purpose[cite: 150, 151, 152, 153, 154].
