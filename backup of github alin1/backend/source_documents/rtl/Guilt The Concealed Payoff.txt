[Guilt The Concealed Payoff]
[Topic: Guilt - Definition and Impact]
GUILT (DEFINITION): As a noun, it is the fact of having committed a specified or implied offense; as a verb, it is the act of making oneself or others feel guilty to induce a specific action[cite: 251, 252]. 
THE ROLE OF GUILT: In the RTL framework, guilt is viewed as a justification for not moving forward in a positive and productive way[cite: 254]. It immobilizes an individual based on past events and serves as a statement that says, "I am suffering; therefore I do not need to take action"[cite: 255, 256]. 
THE DEMAND OF GUILT: Guilt demands that one remains a victim of circumstances[cite: 257]. Within the mood of guilt, the options of being responsible for one's actions or powerfully responding to circumstances to make a contribution do not appear[cite: 258].

[Topic: Myths and Consequences of Guilt]
MYTHS AROUND GUILT:
* Myth 1: Guilt is a valuable exercise for learning and growth[cite: 260].
* Myth 2: Consuming oneself with guilt prevents making the same mistake twice[cite: 261].
CONSEQUENCES: Excessive guilt destroys self-esteem, authenticity, creativity, and growth[cite: 262]. This impact extends beyond the individual to those they interact with and love[cite: 262].

[Topic: Exercise - Inducing Guilt]
