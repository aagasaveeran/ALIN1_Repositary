[Topic: Daily Reflection Journal - Personal Transformation]
PERSONAL TRANSFORMATION REFLECTION: A set of questions designed for daily self-reflection to increase self-awareness and iterative learning[cite: 226, 227].
* Self-Learning: What did I learn about myself today? [cite: 228]
* Behavioral Change: What will I do differently because of what I learned today? [cite: 229]
* Project Application: How will I apply this to my project? [cite: 230]
* Open Reflection: Other reflections? [cite: 231]

[Topic: Daily Reflection Journal - Social Transformation]
SOCIAL TRANSFORMATION REFLECTION: Strategic questions used to align project goals with the RTL framework of systemic and cultural change[cite: 232].
1. Measurable Deliverables: What are the measurable deliverables of my project? [cite: 233]
2. Shifting Norms: What systems, background conversations, and cultural norms do I need to shift for my project to be sustainable? [cite: 234]
3. Sourcing Capacity: How will I bring my inner capacities to bear on this project and source others' inner capacities? [cite: 235]
4. Strategic Pivot: How do I need to change the strategies of my project based on what I've learned so far - what will I do differently? [cite: 236]
