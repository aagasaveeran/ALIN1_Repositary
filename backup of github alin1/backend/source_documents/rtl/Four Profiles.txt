[Four Profiles]
[Topic: My Four Profiles - Overview]
MY FOUR PROFILES: A tool used to distinguish one's inner capacity from social, professional, and personality identities to embrace them all within oneself[cite: 55, 69]. This framework helps individuals recognize the importance of inner potential for creating a just and thriving future where everyone lives with dignity[cite: 70]. It also aids in understanding the four profiles of others to respect diversity and embrace all people[cite: 71].

[Topic: Inner Potential or Inner Capacity (Wisdom Profile)]
INNER POTENTIAL / WISDOM PROFILE: The inner values and capacity, such as dignity, compassion, and equity, that allow a person to see things as they really are[cite: 56, 57]. This profile helps release the grip of fear, anger, and anxiety, leading the individual toward compassionate action in the world[cite: 58].

[Topic: Social Profile]
SOCIAL PROFILE: The set of assumptions and conditioning received from family, religion, education, media, and culture that influence our perception of ourselves and the world[cite: 59, 60]. This profile influences how we perceive ourselves and others in relation to categories like race, gender, caste, tribe, politics, and nationality[cite: 61].

[Topic: Personality Profile]
PERSONALITY PROFILE: An individual's personal style of expression, including unique strengths, limitations, and defense mechanisms[cite: 62, 63]. It represents the unique way a person acts, interacts, and expresses their internal states, such as wisdom, fear, or anxiety[cite: 64].

[Topic: Professional Profile]
PROFESSIONAL PROFILE: The way an individual expresses their professional talents and abilities in the world[cite: 65, 66]. This includes the specific skills one possesses for managing, creating, and manifesting results in a professional context[cite: 67].
