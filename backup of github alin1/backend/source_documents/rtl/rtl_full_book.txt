[Context: Part 2 | Chapter 6 | Topic: The Transformational Results Chain (TRC)]

[Context: Part 2 | Chapter 6 | Topic: Radical Differences from Conventional Planning]

- [TRC Results - Impact]: Equitable and enduring change in human well-being; thriving people and planet.

- [TRC Results - Interim Outcome]: Precursors necessary for change (e.g., establishing a functional facility).
- [TRC Results - Output]: Specific products, technological solutions, or services based on universal values.
- [TRC Results - Inputs]: Resources (money, expertise) procured according to ethical principles.

[Context: Part 2 | Chapter 7 | Topic: The Five Stages of Leadership]
1. Stage 1: Autocratic - Decisions made by oneself. 
   - Healthy: Emergency situations (e.g., saving a child from fire).
   - Unhealthy: Coercive; demanding compliance without context or relatedness.
2. Stage 2: Management/Managers - Relating to rules and roles. 
   - Healthy: Following necessary rules for function (e.g., a referee in a game).
   - Unhealthy: Rules become the end in themselves; bureaucratic obstruction.
3. Stage 3: Pragmatic Leader - Focus on practicality and results. 
   - Healthy: Proactively involving people for a campaign.
   - Unhealthy: The ends justify the means; profit or results at the cost of well-being.
4. Stage 4: Principled Leader/Steward/Mentor - Relying on ethical principles. 
   - Healthy: Reviewing every action in the context of universal values.
   - Unhealthy: Principles become "the truth"; self-righteousness; making others wrong.
5. Stage 5: Perspectivist Leader/Steward - Holding multiple perspectives. 
   - Healthy: Discerning worldviews from mere opinions; valuing diversity and interdependence without compromising values.
